# 🎯 Project Intent & Vision: Nexus

## 1. Core Vision
*Nexus* is designed to bring the deep, character-driven storytelling of classic JRPGs into a highly optimized, browser-accessible 3D environment. It strips away the bloat of modern open-world checklists and tedious base-building, focusing entirely on three pillars: **Intimate Character Dynamics, Seamless Strategic Combat, and Striking Audiovisual Aesthetics.**

## 2. Thematic Goals
*   **Intuition vs. Logic:** Contrasting Ruen's unexplainable, almost mystical connection to the Data Core with the hyper-logical, digitized world of the cyberpunk megacity.
*   **Unconditional Trust:** Showcasing a healthy, supportive romantic relationship (Ruen and Kyle) that isn't bogged down by melodramatic misunderstandings, balanced by the grounding, independent presence of a third party (Yuna).
*   **Environmental Storytelling over UI:** Guiding the player through the world using visual distortions, lighting changes, and environmental glitches rather than heavy UI markers.

## 3. Design Philosophy
*   **Performance First:** By committing to a flat-shaded, low-poly art style without complex textures, the game dedicates its computational budget entirely to dynamic lighting (neon/bloom), particle effects, and seamless transitions, ensuring a premium feel even in a web browser.
*   **Meaningful Grinding:** Combat should never feel like a chore. Upgrading skills and weapons must result in immediate, highly visible upgrades to character models and combat VFX, making the "grind" visually rewarding.
*   **Focused Scope:** A linear, narrative-driven experience within isolated, explorable chunks ensures high-quality pacing and manageable development milestones.
