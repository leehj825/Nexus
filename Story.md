# 📖 Storyline: Nexus

## Overview

*Nexus* is a cyberpunk dark fantasy tale centred on the journey of three individuals. It is not a traditional hero's journey to save the world, but rather an intimate "road movie" through a sprawling, dystopian megacity. The narrative focuses on how they react to an overwhelming, incomprehensible power and how their bonds keep them grounded.

## Act 1: The Pulse in the Dark (The Underbelly)

* **The Discovery:** While taking on a routine scavenging job in the lowest, forgotten sectors of the city, Ruen, Kyle, and Yuna stumble upon a geometric, ancient-looking "Data Core." It is completely alien to the city's standard tech.
* **The Intuition:** As Ruen approaches it, she experiences a terrifying, visceral reaction: a cold intuition that this data is volatile enough to warp the very fabric of their reality. She wants to leave it untouched.
* **The Choice:** Kyle immediately trusts Ruen's instincts, raising his shield and preparing to walk away without questioning her. However, Yuna, viewing the situation with pragmatic detachment, points out that the Mega-Corporation's enforcers are already tracking them. Leaving it behind means handing a world-ending weapon to tyrants.
* **The Inciting Incident:** The trio agrees to secure the Data Core. As they touch it, a massive surge of neon-blue energy pulses through the sector, marking them as targets. Their journey to uncover the origin of this "Shape-Caller" data begins.

## Act 2: Chasing the Glitch (The Ascent)

* **The Resonance:** Ruen realises she can feel the "resonance" of other Data Cores scattered throughout the restricted upper sectors of the megacity. This resonance acts as their compass.
* **Environmental Storytelling:** As they move from chunk to chunk (e.g. from the Slums to the Neon Markets, then to the Industrial Hub), the environments become increasingly corrupted. Holograms glitch into monstrous shapes, and neon signs fracture, visualising the dangerous reality-bending nature of the data.
* **Campfire Skits (Character Growth):** Between sectors, the group sets up camp in abandoned alleys or safe houses.
  * Ruen struggles with the mental toll of the data's whispers.
  * Kyle remains her steadfast anchor, offering quiet comfort and maintaining their gear.
  * Yuna keeps them focused, ensuring their emotional bond doesn't blind them to the harsh realities of their survival.
* **The Revelation:** They discover that the Data Cores are not just weapons, but fragments of a localised singularity: a gateway the ruling Mega-Corporation is trying to forcefully open to rewrite the city's reality in their favour.

## Act 3: The Paradox (The Corporate Spire)

* **The Infiltration:** Guided by the fully synchronised Data Cores, the trio breaches the highest echelons of the city, the Corporate Spire. The environment here is a surreal blend of pristine corporate aesthetics tearing apart at the seams due to the data's raw power.
* **The Climax:** The CEO / main antagonist attempts to merge with the primary singularity. Ruen must push her intuitive connection to the data to its absolute limit to counter the hack, while Kyle intercepts devastating physical and digital attacks. Yuna exploits the system's vulnerabilities with rapid, precise strikes.
* **The Resolution:** They manage to destabilise the singularity, not by destroying the data, but by safely grounding its energy back into the city's defunct grid. The Spire's control over the city is shattered.
* **Epilogue:** The megacity is forever changed, free from the corporation's absolute grip but now wild and unpredictable. The trio, battered but unbroken, packs up their camp and walks into the glowing, glitching horizon, ready for whatever comes next.
