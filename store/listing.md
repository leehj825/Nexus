## Short description (80 characters max)

A neon city story with turn-based battles and a crew of four.

## Full description (4,000 characters max)

Nexus is a story-driven RPG set in a neon megacity that is coming apart.

Four people, one signal, and a city that is coming apart. Follow Jin, Ruen, Kyle and Yuna through the alleys, the markets, an industrial hub and a corporate spire, and find out what the signal wants before the Corporation does.

FIGHT WITH A CREW OF FOUR
- Turn-based battles where each character has a gauge. When it fills, you pick their command.
- Special skills that matter: Stagger breaks enemy shields and wind-ups, Guard protects a friend, and Yuna's Weak Point makes an enemy hit softer.
- Link attacks that unlock as the crew grow closer.
- Enemies with weaknesses. Find them and the battle gets easier.

GROW YOUR CREW
- Level up, pick a perk at each level, and learn new skills.
- Upgrade weapons at camps with scrap from the streets.
- Bond with each other through fights and camp conversations.

A STORY THAT RESPONDS TO YOU
- Choices in conversations change how the crew feel about each other and what happens at the end.
- Each character has their own story.

EXPLORE A CITY
- Side jobs from the people of the streets, a hidden vault, and districts that each feel different.
- Roam freely and fight the patrols you meet.

AFTER THE ENDING
- New Game+ keeps your crew's levels, perks and gear, and starts the story again.
- Challenge mode gives tougher foes and bigger rewards.

SETTINGS
- English and Korean interface (the story dialogue is currently in English).
- Battle speed and auto-battle options.

Nexus is a short, self-contained story. Plays in landscape or portrait.
